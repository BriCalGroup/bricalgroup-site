HOW BRICAL WORKS

A clear question.
A practical system.

We connect the work to a decision, define the scope, and leave behind something that can be understood and maintained.

Discuss a project
01

Step 1

Define the question.

What needs to work better? Who needs the answer? Start with the business need and agree on the sources, scope, deliverables, and acceptance checks.

02

Step 2

Build the useful part.

Create the pages, organize the export, define the report, or document the workflow. Keep the implementation proportional to the problem.

03

Step 3

Check the work.

Review links and contact paths, reconcile calculations, and test the handoff. Identify limitations instead of burying them in a footnote.

04

Step 4

Make the next step clear.

Explain what changed, what the evidence supports, and who owns the next action. Ongoing support is a separate, explicit agreement.

WORKING PRINCIPLES

Clarity in the agreement.
Care in the delivery.

Scope before software.

The deliverables, access, responsibilities, and third-party costs should be clear before the build begins.

Evidence before claims.

Counts, definitions, and assumptions are visible. A chart does not justify a causal claim by itself.

Ownership without confusion.

Core accounts remain under the agreed owner’s control. Documentation makes the handoff more than a password exchange.
