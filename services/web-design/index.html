COMMON QUESTIONS

A few things worth
knowing upfront.

Do you only build WordPress websites?

No. The platform depends on who will update the site and what it needs to do. A lightweight static site can fit a small service business; a publishing-heavy site may need a different setup.

Does an SEO-ready site guarantee search rankings?

No. Technical setup helps search engines access and understand the site, but it does not guarantee indexing, ranking, or traffic. Ongoing content, evidence of your work, and promotion remain separate work.

Will I own my domain and business accounts?

The intended arrangement is for you to retain ownership of your domain and core business accounts. Access, source-file delivery, licenses, and third-party costs should be specified in the agreement.
