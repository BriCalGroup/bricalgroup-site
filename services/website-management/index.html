THE DELIVERABLE

  • Agreed content updates
  • Contact-path and site checks
  • A documented improvement list

THE PURPOSE

Care that you can
actually account for.

Website management is more than holding a hosting account. The useful work is keeping information accurate, checking the customer journey, reviewing agreed performance measures, and making changes with a reason behind them.

A GOOD STARTING POINT WHEN

  • Your service details or content change regularly.
  • Nobody consistently checks forms, links, or mobile layouts.
  • You want regular improvements without managing every technical task.

WHAT A SCOPED PROJECT CAN INCLUDE

Concrete work.
A clear handoff.

01

Content & small changes

Updates to agreed pages, service information, and website content within a defined monthly allowance.

02

Site & inquiry checks

Scheduled checks of important links, contact routes, and key pages. Review applicable technical maintenance for the chosen platform.

03

Performance review

A focused review of agreed indicators, with clear distinctions between visits, inquiries, bookings, and revenue where that data exists.

04

Prioritized improvements

A record of completed work and proposed changes. Larger development projects and experiments are scoped separately.

This is not an unlimited-edit or 24/7 emergency-response promise. Response windows, platform responsibilities, backup practices, subscriptions, and work limits are agreed in writing.

THE BRICAL APPROACH

A monthly relationship.
Not a mystery fee.

Each care arrangement should state what gets checked, what gets updated, and what gets reported. Some sites need only periodic care. Others need recurring content and measurement. The plan should reflect the actual workload.

How we work

Measure before declaring a win.

A changed headline and a busier month do not automatically establish cause and effect. Meaningful experiments need adequate traffic, consistent measurement, and a clear decision rule.

COMMON QUESTIONS

A few things worth
knowing upfront.

Can you manage a site you did not build?

Possibly. It starts with a review of the platform, account ownership, current issues, and what access is available. Support is only offered after the requirements are understood.

Is hosting the same as website management?

No. Hosting makes the site available online. Management covers the agreed work to keep its content, contact paths, and supporting processes useful. Hosting and subscriptions should be itemized.

Can I request a major redesign through a care plan?

A redesign, new integration, or substantial expansion needs its own scope. Small updates can be included, but the boundary should be clear before the plan starts.
