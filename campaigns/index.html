COMMON QUESTIONS

Scope comes before
access.

Who is this service for?

Campaign organizations, consulting firms, field vendors, and other teams that need operational reporting support. The scope and data requirements determine fit.

What should we share in an initial inquiry?

Describe the reporting problem, the program structure, and the kind of exports available. Do not send voter files, individual-level sensitive data, credentials, or confidential records in an initial email.

Is this separate from the business services?

It is a specialist practice within Brical. The same emphasis on reliable records and practical reporting applies, but the scope is designed around field operations.
