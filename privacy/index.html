WEBSITE INFORMATION

Privacy, in plain language.

How this website handles information, and a few important limits to keep in mind.

Updated September 14, 2026

Who operates this website

This website presents the services of Brical Group, a web, data, and operations practice based in the Rio Grande Valley, Texas. For website or privacy questions, email hello@bricalgroup.com .

Contact inquiries

The contact page prepares an email draft on your device. It does not send your entries to a website form server or save them in browser storage. You choose whether to send the draft through your email provider. Copying an inquiry places its text on your device’s clipboard.

If you email Brical, the information in that message is processed by the relevant email providers and used to respond to your inquiry and discuss possible services. Do not include sensitive personal information, passwords, or confidential datasets in an initial message.

Technical connection information

The website is delivered through Cloudflare Pages. Hosting and security services may process technical information such as IP addresses, requested URLs, timestamps, and security events to deliver and protect the site. Cloudflare describes its practices in its privacy policy .

Cookies and measurement

The website code does not include advertising pixels, session-replay tools, or a third-party marketing analytics integration. It does not use browser storage for the inquiry builder. Hosting or security features may use information needed to operate the site. This notice should be revisited if additional tracking, forms, booking tools, or integrations are introduced.

Business information and demonstrations

The sample report and its downloadable CSV contain fictional data. They are not client results, testimonials, forecasts, or guarantees. Website content is general information; the scope, responsibilities, and fees for any service are established in a separate agreement.

External links and third-party services

Linked websites and your chosen email provider operate under their own terms and privacy practices. Their inclusion is not a claim that Brical controls those services.

Questions or requests

For questions about information you have sent to Brical, email hello@bricalgroup.com . Include enough context to identify the correspondence without sending unnecessary sensitive information.
